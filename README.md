 # 🛍️ Retail Performance & E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end Business Intelligence (BI) dashboard connected directly to a relational **PostgreSQL** database to analyze multi-year retail performance, customer behavior, logistical delivery efficiency, and payment trends.

---

## 🚀 Skills & Competencies Demonstrated

This project showcases a powerful combination of database management, SQL querying, and business intelligence reporting:

### **Technical Skills**
- **Database Management & SQL:** Extracted, queried, and managed large-scale transactional datasets directly using **PostgreSQL** (handling tables for orders, customers, payments, products, and sellers).
- **Data Modeling & Transformation:** Structuring relational schemas for high-performance dashboard reporting.
- **Data Visualization & Dashboard Design:** Designing clean, executive-ready Power BI layouts using key performance cards, geographic maps, tree maps, and bar charts[cite: 1].
- **DAX & Calculations:** Computing custom business metrics like Average Order Value (AOV), On-Time Delivery Percentage, and category distributions[cite: 1].

### **Business & Domain Skills**
- **Sales & Revenue Analytics:** Tracking total revenue performance, revenue trends by year/month, and top-performing product categories[cite: 1].
- **Logistics & Supply Chain Analysis:** Evaluating on-time delivery rates (~93.23%) and tracking delivery delays across customer states[cite: 1].
- **Customer Segmentation & Sentiment Analysis:** Analyzing customer review ratings (positive vs. negative shares) and regional geographic distribution[cite: 1].
- **Financial Operations Analysis:** Monitoring payment method shares (Credit Card ~76% vs. Boleto ~19%) and installment behaviors[cite: 1].

---

## 📊 Key Project Metrics & Performance
- **Total Revenue:** 14.21M+[cite: 1]
- **Total Orders:** 98,670+[cite: 1]
- **Customer Base:** 95,420+ unique customers[cite: 1]
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** 144.01[cite: 1]
- **On-Time Delivery Rate:** 93.23%[cite: 1]
- **Customer Satisfaction:** 4.03 / 5 Average Rating (76.97% Positive Reviews)[cite: 1]

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights
- **Top Product Categories:** Categories like *Health & Beauty*, *Watches & Gifts*, and *Bed, Bath & Table* lead the market in total revenue generation[cite: 1].
- **Payment Preferences:** Credit cards dominate transaction volume at roughly 76%, followed by Boleto transactions at ~19%[cite: 1].
- **Logistics Efficiency:** Successfully tracks an on-time delivery rate of 93.23%, with granular analysis monitoring late transit days by category and state[cite: 1].

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
- **Database:** PostgreSQL[cite: 1]
- **Data Visualization:** Power BI[cite: 1]
- **Querying & Management:** SQL, pgAdmin / DBeaver
- **Version Control:** Git & GitHub
